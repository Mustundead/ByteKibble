安装 / Install
将 ByteKibble 拖到 Applications（应用程序），然后从“应用程序”打开。
Drag ByteKibble onto Applications, then launch it from Applications.
替换旧版本前请先退出应用。订阅和偏好设置会保留。
Quit the previous version before replacing it. Your subscriptions and preferences are retained.
安装后可推出磁盘映像。菜单栏图标用于打开流量面板。
Eject this disk after installation. Open the traffic panel from the menu bar icon.
